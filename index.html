How to play

Guess where in the world each postcard was sent from.

  1. Read the cluesThey start vague and end at a famous landmark.
  2. Drop a pinClick the map to place it. Tap a city dot to snap to it.
  3. Send your guessFewer clues used means more points.
  4. Collect itLand within 100 km to add the postcard to your Collection.

Map: scroll or pinch to zoom, drag to pan. It wraps around, so keep going past the edge.

Daily: 5 new postcards every day at midnight Eastern. Missed one? Check the Archive.
